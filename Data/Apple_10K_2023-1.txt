Business Overview

Company Background

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, accessories, and related services. The fiscal year is the 52- or 53-week period ending on the last Saturday of September.

Products

iPhone: iOS smartphones (iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15, iPhone 14, iPhone 13, iPhone SE)

Mac: macOS personal computers (MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, Mac Pro)

iPad: iPadOS tablets (iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad, iPad mini)

Wearables, Home, and Accessories:

Apple Watch (Ultra 2, Series 9, SE)

AirPods (AirPods, AirPods Pro, AirPods Max, Beats)

Apple TV, HomePod, HomePod mini

Apple-branded and third-party accessories

Services

Advertising: Licensing and Apple’s own ad platforms.

AppleCare: Paid service and support offerings.

Cloud Services: Cross-device cloud storage.

Digital Content: App Store, Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, Apple Music, Apple News+, Apple TV+.

Payment Services: Apple Card and Apple Pay.

Segments

Americas: North and South America

Europe: Including India, Middle East, Africa

Greater China: China mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan

Japan

Rest of Asia Pacific: Includes Australia

Markets and Distribution

Customers include consumers, SMBs, education, enterprise, and government markets. Sales channels: direct (retail and online) and indirect (carriers, wholesalers, resellers).

Competition

Apple competes globally with companies emphasizing pricing, product features, ecosystem, design, and service.

Supply Chain

Some components are single-source.

Custom components often used.

Supply chain risks include shortages and pricing fluctuations.

Research and Development

Apple focuses on continual innovation via R&D, IP licensing, and acquisitions.

Intellectual Property

Apple owns a wide range of patents, trademarks, copyrights, and designs globally.

Business Seasonality

Sales peak in Q1 due to holiday demand.

Human Capital

Approx. 161,000 full-time employees (as of Sept 30, 2023)

Focus on diversity, inclusion, and safety

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Macroeconomic Risks

Global economic conditions (inflation, recession, interest rates)

International operations and supply chain exposure

Adverse impacts on demand and supply

Geopolitical & Environmental Risks

Political disputes, trade restrictions, wars, natural disasters, public health crises

Regulatory barriers in key markets (e.g., U.S.-China tensions)

Competitive Risks

Aggressive pricing, short product life cycles, evolving standards

Competitors may imitate Apple or sell at low/no profit

Product & Supply Chain Risks

Single-source suppliers for key components

Custom component constraints

Delays and shortages impact production

Legal and Compliance Risks

Antitrust scrutiny globally

Increasing regulations on data privacy, labor, environment, etc.

Pending legislation (e.g., EU Digital Markets Act)

Financial Risks

Fluctuating foreign exchange rates

Dependence on iPhone as key revenue driver

Gross margin volatility

Exposure to credit risk from resellers and suppliers

Forward-Looking Statements

Statements regarding future performance, risks, and uncertainties.
